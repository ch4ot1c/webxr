AR/MR Physics Sandbox

Right Controller (Main)

• Trigger: Fire weapon (Balls/Lasers)
• B Button: Cycle Modes (Balls → Lasers → Paddle → Shield → Sword)
• A Button: Toggle Laser Pointer / Cycle Sword Stances
• Grip (Hold): Activate Gesture Spellcasting

Left Controller (Offhand)

• Trigger (Hold & Release): Grab and throw physics balls
• X Button: Toggle Avatar (Head/Hands) Visibility
• Y Button: Toggle Shield Mode (Absorb vs Deflect)
• Thumbstick Click: Toggle Dummy Training Launcher
• Grip: Toggle Room Scan Mesh Visibility

Motion Actions

• Eat/Drink: Bring controller to mouth (tilt sideways to drink)
• Spellcasting: Hold Right Grip and trace glowing orbs to cast spells
• Tennis Paddle: Swing fast to spike the ball (Underhand toss with left hand for moon-gravity)
