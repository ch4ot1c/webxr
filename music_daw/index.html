Quest 3 AR Audio Studio

1. Open in Meta Quest Browser & Enter AR.
2. PLAY & PERCUSSION: Pinch/tap the 16-pad sampler, 4-octave piano keyboard, customizable bongo kits, or swipe through the interactive harp.
3. KINETIC SHAKER: Pinch near the red egg shaker to pick it up and shake it physically in space.
4. STUDIO TOOLS: Use the in-VR UI desk to toggle the metronome, record into 4 independent looper tracks, or route live vocals via microphone.
5. DAW & EXPORT: View real-time piano rolls and audio waveforms floating above your workspace, and automatically export multi-track WebM stems and JSON telemetry upon completion.
